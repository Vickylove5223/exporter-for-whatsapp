How it works

From chat to file in three steps

Open WhatsApp Web

Go to web.whatsapp.com and log in. Then click the extension icon to open the side panel.

Pick your chats

Choose chats or contacts, then "All time" or a date range, and whether to include media.

Export

Pick a format and hit Export. Your file lands in your Downloads folder, already named for today.

Private by design

Your messages never leave your browser.

  • No servers, no tracking. The extension makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no cloud.
  • Minimal permissions. It only runs on web.whatsapp.com, and only reads chats when you press Export.
  • No account, no login. Install it and use it. There's nothing to sign up for.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid plans, no premium features and no export limits.

Are my messages uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser and saved straight to your computer. The extension has no server and sends no data anywhere.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Any account you can open on web.whatsapp.com works, including WhatsApp Business.

Can I export photos, voice notes and videos?

Yes. Turn on the media you want in the export settings. Media that WhatsApp Web has loaded is saved into a ZIP alongside your transcript, and duplicates are stored only once.

Why are some very old messages missing?

The extension can only export what WhatsApp Web can load from your phone. For very long chats it scrolls back for up to two minutes per chat, then saves what it has and tells you the export is partial.

Which file format should I pick?

CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, JSON for developers and AI tools, HTML to read chats like WhatsApp in your browser, PDF to print or archive, TXT for a simple plain-text copy.

Is this an official WhatsApp product?

No. It's an independent tool and isn't affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.
